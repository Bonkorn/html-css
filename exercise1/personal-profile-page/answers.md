# Personal profile page: my answers

## 1. Which line controls the browser tab title?
`<title>` inside `<head>`

## 2. Which part of your HTML contains the visible page content?
`<body>`

## 3. Why did you choose `<ul>` for one list and `<ol>` for the other?
i use `<ul>` when the order of the items doesn't matter; i use `<ol>` when the order of the items is important, such as step-by-step, ranking, etc

## 4. What does the alt text of your image tell the reader?
the screenreader will verbally describes what does my image look like or what is it shown in the image, to readers cannot see

## 5. Which two attribute values connect your label and your input?
`<label for="phonenumber">Phone Number</label>` and `<input id="phonenumber" type="tel" placeholder="0123456789">`
